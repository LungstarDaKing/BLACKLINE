# characters/police

**What belongs here:** South African Police Service (SAPS) style officers for later missions. Use a fictional or generic uniform design unless an authentic reference and licence situation is confirmed (real insignia are a legal question, see `docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md`).

**Format:** GLB preferred. **Skeleton:** REQUIRED (shared humanoid). **Animations:** Idle, Walk, Run, Aim, Fire, Hit, Death (in place).
**Orientation and scale:** faces -Z, feet at Y = 0, 1 unit = 1 m, about 1.8 m. Budget about 6,000 to 10,000 triangles, textures 512 to 1024.
**Record keeping:** source, author, licence, date in `docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md`; status in `docs/ASSET_CATALOGUE.md`.
